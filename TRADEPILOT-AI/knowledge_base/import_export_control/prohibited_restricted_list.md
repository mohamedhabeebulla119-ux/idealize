# 2026 Restricted and Prohibited Items Guide

**Issued By:** The Department of Import and Export Control (DIEC)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

---

## Introduction

The Department of Import and Export Control (DIEC) is responsible for regulating the flow of goods into and out of [Your Country Name/Jurisdiction]. This guide outlines categories of items that are either strictly prohibited from entering or leaving our borders, or are restricted and require specific licenses, permits, or authorizations from relevant authorities.

This guide is issued to ensure national security, protect public health and safety, safeguard our environment, uphold international obligations, and prevent illegal trade. Importers, exporters, and travelers are strongly advised to review this guide carefully and ensure full compliance with all regulations. Ignorance of the law is not an excuse for non-compliance, and violations will result in severe penalties, including fines, confiscation of goods, and imprisonment.

---

## Section 1: Strictly Prohibited Items

**Definition:** These items are absolutely forbidden from being imported into or exported from [Your Country Name/Jurisdiction] under any circumstances. Possession, trade, or transit of these items is illegal and will result in immediate legal action.

1.  **Narcotics and Illegal Drugs:**
    *   **Examples:** Heroin, cocaine, crystal methamphetamine, MDMA, opium, cannabis (unless specifically authorized for medicinal or scientific research by the Ministry of Health).
    *   **Reason:** Public health and safety, prevention of illicit drug trade.

2.  **Unauthorized Firearms, Ammunition, and Explosives:**
    *   **Examples:** Automatic weapons, military-grade explosives, armor-piercing ammunition, homemade explosive devices, rocket launchers, chemical weapons. (Excludes legitimate, licensed imports by authorized government agencies or highly regulated entities with specific, prior approval from the Ministry of Defense/Interior).
    *   **Reason:** National security, public safety, counter-terrorism.

3.  **Counterfeit Goods and Pirated Materials:**
    *   **Examples:** Fake branded apparel, accessories, electronics, software, pirated DVDs, CDs, and books.
    *   **Reason:** Protection of intellectual property rights, prevention of fraud.

4.  **Hazardous Waste and Toxic Chemicals (Unlicensed):**
    *   **Examples:** Industrial toxic waste, radioactive waste (without specific permits), specific ozone-depleting substances (e.g., CFCs), polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs) without proper authorization.
    *   **Reason:** Environmental protection, public health, adherence to international treaties (e.g., Basel Convention).

5.  **Live Animals and Products of Endangered Species (CITES Appendix I):**
    *   **Examples:** Ivory, rhino horn, tiger parts, great ape species, certain exotic birds, pangolin scales, sea turtle products.
    *   **Reason:** Wildlife conservation, adherence to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES).

6.  **Pornographic or Obscene Materials:**
    *   **Examples:** Child pornography, extremist propaganda materials, any content deemed obscene or offensive to public morality by prevailing standards.
    *   **Reason:** Public morality, protection of minors, national security.

## Section 2: Restricted Items Requiring Special Licenses/Permits

**Definition:** These items may be imported or exported, but only with prior authorization in the form of specific licenses, permits, or approvals from the relevant government ministry or regulatory body. Conditions for issuance are stringent and vary by item.

1.  **Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs/Drones):**
    *   **Requirement:** License from the Civil Aviation Authority and/or Ministry of Defense, depending on specifications and intended use (commercial, recreational, or strategic).
    *   **Reason:** Airspace safety, national security, privacy concerns.

2.  **Live Animals, Plants, and Plant Products:**
    *   **Requirement:** Import/export permits from the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock and/or Department of Quarantine, health certificates, phytosanitary certificates.
    *   **Examples:** Livestock, poultry, seeds, fresh fruits, vegetables, cut flowers, specific timber.
    *   **Reason:** Biosecurity, prevention of disease, control of invasive species.

3.  **Pharmaceuticals, Medicines, and Medical Devices:**
    *   **Requirement:** Licenses from the Ministry of Health or National Drug Regulatory Authority. May include import permits for specific controlled substances, prescription drugs, or advanced medical equipment.
    *   **Examples:** Prescription medications, vaccines, controlled substances (e.g., certain pain relievers, psychotropic drugs), X-ray machines, MRI scanners.
    *   **Reason:** Public health and safety, quality control, prevention of misuse.

4.  **Radioactive Materials and Nuclear Substances:**
    *   **Requirement:** Permits from the National Atomic Energy Commission or equivalent regulatory body, adherence to strict international safety standards.
    *   **Examples:** Radioactive isotopes for medical or industrial use, depleted uranium, nuclear waste (for specific reprocessing or disposal).
    *   **Reason:** Public safety, security, non-proliferation, environmental protection.

5.  **Dual-Use Technologies and High-Tech Equipment:**
    *   **Requirement:** Export Control License from the Ministry of Trade and Industry or dedicated National Export Control Authority. End-user certificates often required.
    *   **Examples:** Advanced encryption software, precision manufacturing tools, specialized sensors, certain chemicals with potential military applications, high-performance computing equipment.
    *   **Reason:** National security, prevention of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, adherence to international export control regimes.

6.  **Precious Metals and Stones:**
    *   **Requirement:** Declarations and permits from the Ministry of Finance or Central Bank, especially for large quantities, uncut stones, or specific origins. Compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations is mandatory.
    *   **Examples:** Uncut diamonds, gold bullion exceeding specified weight/value limits, raw platinum.
    *   **Reason:** Economic stability, prevention of illicit trade, anti-money laundering.

## Important Notes:

*   **This guide is not exhaustive.** Other items may be restricted or prohibited based on evolving national and international regulations, trade agreements, and security concerns.
*   **Always verify:** It is the responsibility of the importer/exporter to consult the most current legislation, contact the relevant regulatory bodies, and obtain all necessary documentation *before* attempting to import or export any goods.
*   **Penalties:** Violations of import/export controls can lead to severe penalties, including seizure of goods, substantial fines, and criminal prosecution.
*   **Temporary Restrictions:** Additional temporary restrictions or prohibitions may be enacted by the government in response to emergencies, outbreaks, or specific security threats.
