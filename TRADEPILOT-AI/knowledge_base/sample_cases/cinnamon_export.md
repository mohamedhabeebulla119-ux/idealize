# Circular: Export Licensing and Certification for Cinnamon (HS Code 0906) to Dubai

**Issuing Authority:** Department of Import and Export Control, Sri Lanka
**Date of Issue:** June 20, 2026
**Subject:** Export Requirements for Ceylon Cinnamon

## 1. Applicable Goods
This circular applies to all export shipments of Cinnamon and Cinnamon-tree flowers classified under the Harmonized System (HS) Code **0906**, specifically targeted for export to the United Arab Emirates (Dubai).

## 2. Required Permits and Certifications
To export cinnamon to Dubai, the following documents are mandatory:
*   **Export License:** Required from the Department of Import and Export Control for commercial quantities.
*   **Phytosanitary Certificate:** Mandatory. Must be obtained from the National Plant Quarantine Service (NPQS) of Sri Lanka to certify that the shipment is free from pests and diseases.
*   **Certificate of Origin (CoO):** Issued by the Department of Commerce or the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce, proving the goods are authentic Ceylon Cinnamon.
*   **Quality Certificate:** (Optional but recommended) from the Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI).

## 3. Procedures
1.  **Registration:** The exporter must be registered with the Export Development Board (EDB) of Sri Lanka.
2.  **Application for Phytosanitary Certificate:** Submit samples of the cinnamon consignment to the NPQS at least 7 days prior to shipment.
3.  **Customs Declaration (CUSDEC):** File the CUSDEC electronically via the ASYCUDA system.
4.  **Cargo Examination:** Subject the goods to standard Sri Lanka Customs examination at the port of departure.

## 4. Authorities Involved
*   Department of Import and Export Control
*   National Plant Quarantine Service (NPQS)
*   Sri Lanka Customs
*   Export Development Board (EDB)
