# National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) of Sri Lanka
## Guidelines for the Importation and Registration of Medical Devices (HS Code 9018)

**Date of Issue:** October 26, 2023
**Version:** 1.0
**Issued By:** National Medicines Regulatory Authority of Sri Lanka

---

### 1. Introduction and Purpose

The National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) of Sri Lanka, established under the National Medicines Regulatory Authority Act, No. 5 of 2015, is responsible for regulating the manufacture, import, sale, and distribution of medicines, medical devices, and borderline products in Sri Lanka.

These guidelines are issued to ensure that all Medical Devices imported into Sri Lanka meet the required standards of safety, quality, efficacy, and performance. This document outlines the mandatory NMRA registration process for medical devices, requirements for importer licensing, necessary quality management system certifications (ISO 13485), and considerations for import tax exemptions, specifically for devices falling under HS Code 9018.

All importers, manufacturers, and distributors of medical devices are required to adhere strictly to these guidelines and all other relevant laws and regulations of Sri Lanka.

### 2. Scope

These guidelines apply to all Medical Devices as defined by the NMRA, classified under the Harmonized System (HS) Code 9018, which broadly covers "Instruments and appliances used in medical, surgical, dental or veterinary sciences, including scintigraphic apparatus, other electromedical apparatus and sight-testing instruments." This includes, but is not limited to:

*   Surgical instruments
*   Diagnostic imaging apparatus
*   Patient monitoring systems
*   Implantable devices
*   In-vitro diagnostic devices
*   Dental instruments and equipment
*   Electromedical equipment

### 3. Key Definitions

*   **Medical Device:** Any instrument, apparatus, implement, machine, contrivance, implant, in vitro reagent or other similar or related article, including a component part or accessory which is intended for use in the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of disease in man or animals.
*   **Manufacturer:** Any person or entity that designs, manufactures, assembles, processes, or packages a medical device.
*   **Importer/Local Agent:** Any person or entity in Sri Lanka responsible for bringing medical devices into the country and acting on behalf of an overseas manufacturer for registration and post-market activities.
*   **NMRA Registration Certificate:** A certificate issued by the NMRA acknowledging that a specific medical device meets the regulatory requirements for import and sale in Sri Lanka.
*   **Importer License:** A license issued by the NMRA authorizing an entity to import medical devices into Sri Lanka.
*   **ISO 13485:** An internationally recognized standard for quality management systems specific to the medical devices industry.

### 4. NMRA Medical Device Registration Process

All medical devices imported into Sri Lanka MUST be registered with the NMRA prior to importation and distribution. The registration process involves a comprehensive evaluation to ensure the device's safety, quality, and performance.

#### 4.1. Steps for Device Registration:

1.  **Appointment of Local Agent:**
    *   Overseas manufacturers must appoint a local company (importer) based in Sri Lanka to act as their authorized representative/local agent for all regulatory matters.
    *   A formal **Power of Attorney** or **Agency Agreement** explicitly defining the responsibilities of the local agent must be submitted.

2.  **Submission of Application:**
    *   The appointed local agent must submit a formal application for medical device registration to the NMRA on the prescribed forms (available on the NMRA website).
    *   The application must be accompanied by a comprehensive dossier containing all required technical, quality, and administrative documents.

3.  **Dossier Requirements:** The application dossier typically includes, but is not limited to, the following documents:
    *   **Administrative Documents:**
        *   Completed NMRA application form.
        *   Letter of authorization/Power of Attorney from the manufacturer to the local agent.
        *   Manufacturer's Business Registration Certificate.
        *   Local Agent's Business Registration Certificate and NMRA Importer's License (see Section 6).
        *   Payment of prescribed application fees.
    *   **Manufacturer and Device Information:**
        *   Full name and address of the manufacturer (including manufacturing site).
        *   Full name, model, and catalogue number of the medical device.
        *   Intended use of the device and its indications/contraindications.
        *   Device classification (e.g., Class I, IIa, IIb, III, based on risk, as per international guidelines adopted by NMRA).
    *   **Quality Management System Documentation:**
        *   **Valid ISO 13485 Certificate** of the manufacturing facility from an internationally recognized and accredited certification body (see Section 5).
        *   Proof of compliance with other relevant international standards (e.g., CE Mark, FDA approval, TGA, Health Canada, MHLW Japan, etc.), if applicable.
    *   **Product Technical Documentation:**
        *   Free Sales Certificate (FSC) or Certificate of Exportability from the regulatory authority of the country of origin.
        *   Declaration of Conformity issued by the manufacturer.
        *   Detailed technical specifications, design drawings, and materials used.
        *   Risk Management Report (e.g., ISO 14971 compliance).
        *   Clinical Evaluation Report (CER) or performance evaluation data, especially for higher-risk devices.
        *   Biological compatibility data (e.g., ISO 10993 series), if applicable.
        *   Sterilization validation data (for sterile devices, e.g., ISO 11135, ISO 11137).
        *   Software validation documentation (for devices incorporating software).
        *   Labeling, packaging artwork, and Instructions For Use (IFU) in English. Local language versions may be required for certain devices.
    *   **Post-Market Surveillance Data:**
        *   Summary of adverse event reports, recalls, and field safety corrective actions undertaken in other markets (if any).
        *   Commitment to comply with NMRA's post-market surveillance requirements.

4.  **Evaluation and Review:**
    *   The NMRA reviews the submitted dossier for completeness, accuracy, and compliance with local and international standards.
    *   Additional information or clarification may be requested from the local agent or manufacturer.
    *   For certain high-risk devices or new manufacturers, the NMRA may conduct or request an audit of the manufacturing facility or assessment of the local agent's capabilities.

5.  **Approval and Issuance of Registration Certificate:**
    *   Upon satisfactory review and approval, the NMRA will issue a Medical Device Registration Certificate, valid for a specified period (e.g., 5 years), which is subject to renewal.
    *   The device will be listed in the NMRA's official register of approved medical devices.

#### 4.2. Post-Registration Requirements:

*   **Vigilance and Adverse Event Reporting:** The local agent is responsible for promptly reporting any adverse events, field safety corrective actions, or recalls to the NMRA.
*   **Variations and Amendments:** Any significant changes to the registered device, manufacturer, or local agent details must be notified to and approved by the NMRA.
*   **Renewal:** The registration certificate must be renewed before its expiry, following the prescribed renewal process and fees.

### 5. ISO 13485 Certification

**ISO 13485:2016 (Medical devices – Quality management systems – Requirements for regulatory purposes)** is a critical requirement for manufacturers of medical devices seeking registration in Sri Lanka.

*   **Purpose:** This international standard specifies requirements for a quality management system where an organization needs to demonstrate its ability to provide medical devices and related services that consistently meet customer and applicable regulatory requirements.
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** The NMRA mandates that all manufacturing facilities producing medical devices for import into Sri Lanka must possess a valid ISO 13485 certificate. This certificate must be issued by an internationally recognized and accredited certification body.
*   **Verification:** A copy of the valid ISO 13485 certificate, along with audit reports (if requested), must be included in the registration dossier. The NMRA reserves the right to verify the authenticity and scope of the certification.
*   **Local Agent's Role:** While the ISO 13485 applies to the manufacturer, the local agent must ensure that the manufacturer maintains compliance with this standard throughout the product's lifecycle in the Sri Lankan market.

### 6. Importer Licensing

In addition to registering each medical device, the local entity responsible for importing medical devices into Sri Lanka must hold a valid **Importer's License** issued by the NMRA.

#### 6.1. Process for Importer Licensing:

1.  **Application Submission:** The prospective importer submits an application to the NMRA on the prescribed form.
2.  **Documentation:** Required documents typically include:
    *   Company Business Registration Certificate.
    *   Details of company directors/owners.
    *   Organizational chart and key personnel qualifications (e.g., pharmacists, biomedical engineers).
    *   Proof of adequate storage facilities (e.g., warehousing, cold chain management, controlled environment) compliant with Good Storage Practices (GSP) and Good Distribution Practices (GDP).
    *   Details of transport and distribution arrangements.
    *   Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for handling, storage, distribution, complaint handling, and recalls.
    *   Payment of prescribed application fees.
3.  **Inspection:** The NMRA will conduct an inspection of the applicant's premises (warehouses, offices) to verify compliance with GSP/GDP and other regulatory requirements.
4.  **Approval and Issuance of License:** Upon satisfactory assessment and inspection, the NMRA will issue an Importer's License, valid for a specific period and subject to renewal.

#### 6.2. Responsibilities of Licensed Importers:

*   Ensure all imported medical devices are registered with the NMRA.
*   Maintain proper storage conditions as per manufacturer's recommendations and NMRA guidelines.
*   Implement a robust complaint handling and recall system.
*   Report adverse events promptly to the NMRA.
*   Maintain accurate records of all import, sales, and distribution activities.
*   Adhere to all applicable NMRA regulations and guidelines.

### 7. Import Tax Exemptions

Medical devices are generally subject to applicable customs duties, Value Added Tax (VAT), and other levies as per Sri Lankan customs regulations. However, specific provisions for tax exemptions or concessions may exist under certain circumstances.

#### 7.1. Eligibility for Exemption Considerations:

While the NMRA does not directly grant tax exemptions, it plays a crucial role in the process by verifying the medical necessity and regulatory compliance of devices. Potential scenarios for tax exemption consideration include:

*   **Government Procurements:** Medical devices imported by government institutions (e.g., Ministry of Health, government hospitals) for public health services.
*   **Donor-Funded Projects:** Devices imported under specific grants or aid projects by international organizations or foreign governments.
*   **Devices Not Locally Manufactured:** For specific devices deemed critical and not manufactured domestically.
*   **Special Public Health Programs:** Devices designated for use in national health programs or emergencies.

#### 7.2. Process for Seeking Tax Exemptions:

1.  **NMRA Registration:** The medical device(s) must be fully registered and approved by the NMRA.
2.  **Application to Relevant Authority:** The importer or procuring entity (e.g., Ministry of Health, government hospital) must formally apply to the **Ministry of Finance** or **Sri Lanka Customs** for tax exemption.
3.  **NMRA Recommendation:** The application for exemption must typically be supported by a **recommendation letter or approval from the NMRA**, confirming:
    *   The medical device is registered and approved for import in Sri Lanka.
    *   The device's intended use aligns with public health objectives or the specific program.
    *   The quantity requested is reasonable for the stated purpose.
4.  **Customs Review and Granting:** Sri Lanka Customs, in consultation with the Ministry of Finance, will review the application and supporting documents, including the NMRA's recommendation, to determine eligibility and grant any applicable tax exemptions.
5.  **Specific Conditions:** Any granted exemptions may come with specific conditions, such as restrictions on sale, transfer, or use.

**Note:** Importers are strongly advised to consult with Sri Lanka Customs and the Ministry of Finance regarding the latest tax policies and exemption procedures. The NMRA's role is primarily regulatory approval and recommendation, not the granting of financial concessions.

### 8. Compliance and Enforcement

The NMRA reserves the right to inspect any premises, records, or activities related to the import, storage, distribution, and sale of medical devices. Non-compliance with these guidelines, the NMRA Act, or any other relevant regulations may result in:

*   Refusal or revocation of device registration.
*   Refusal or revocation of importer's license.
*   Seizure and destruction of non-compliant devices.
*   Imposition of penalties and fines.
*   Legal prosecution as per the NMRA Act, No. 5 of 2015.
