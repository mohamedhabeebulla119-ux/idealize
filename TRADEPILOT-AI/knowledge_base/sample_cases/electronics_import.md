# Sri Lanka Customs Guideline: Importation of Consumer Electronic Devices (HS Code 8543)

**Authority:** Sri Lanka Customs
**Subject:** Import Procedures and Tariff Structure for Electronic Devices

## 1. Overview
This guideline outlines the regulatory requirements, approvals, and tax structures applicable to the importation of consumer electronic devices classified under HS Code **8543** (Electrical machines and apparatus, having individual functions, not specified or included elsewhere in this Chapter).

## 2. Required Approvals
Importers of telecommunication-capable electronic devices (e.g., smartphones, tablets with cellular connectivity, radio-frequency emitters) must obtain prior approval before customs clearance.
*   **TRCSL Type Approval:** The Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) must issue a Type Approval certificate for the specific make and model of the device.
*   **Import License:** For commercial quantities, an import license from the Department of Import and Export Control may be required depending on the current gazette notifications.

## 3. Applicable Duties and Taxes
The following taxes and levies are generally applied to HS Code 8543, calculated on the CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) value:
*   **Customs Import Duty (CID):** Typically ranges from 0% to 15% depending on the specific item sub-category.
*   **Value Added Tax (VAT):** 18% (as of current standard rates), calculated on (CIF + CID + PAL + CESS).
*   **Ports and Airports Development Levy (PAL):** Usually 5% or 10%.
*   **CESS:** Applicable based on specific weight or ad valorem percentage as published by the Export Development Board.

## 4. Import Documentation
*   Commercial Invoice
*   Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB)
*   Packing List
*   TRCSL Approval Document
*   Customs Declaration (CUSDEC)

*Note: All declarations must be submitted electronically via the ASYCUDA World system by a registered Customs House Agent (CHA).*
