# Official Procedure for Exporting Cut and Polished Gemstones (HS Code 7103) from Sri Lanka to the USA

---

**NATIONAL GEM AND JEWELLERY AUTHORITY OF SRI LANKA**

**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Reference:** NGJA/EXP/PROC/7103/USA/V1.0

## Subject: Official Procedure for Exportation of Cut and Polished Gemstones (HS Code 7103) to the United States of America

This document outlines the official procedure for Sri Lankan registered exporters to legally export cut and polished gemstones (classified under HS Code 7103) to the United States of America. Adherence to this procedure is mandatory to ensure compliance with national regulations and international trade standards.

---

### **I. Introduction and Scope**

The National Gem and Jewellery Authority (NGJA) is the apex body responsible for regulating, promoting, and developing the gem and jewellery industry in Sri Lanka, as mandated by the National Gem and Jewellery Authority Act No. 50 of 1993. This procedure details the steps required for the legal export of finished gemstones to the USA, ensuring transparency, fair valuation, and compliance with all statutory requirements.

### **II. Pre-Requisites for Exporters**

1.  **NGJA Registration:** The exporting entity must be a registered gem exporter with the National Gem and Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka.
2.  **Valid Business Registration:** The exporter must possess a valid business registration in Sri Lanka.
3.  **Tax Identification:** Registered for applicable taxes (e.g., VAT, Income Tax) with the Department of Inland Revenue.
4.  **Commercial Transaction:** A confirmed order or sales agreement with a legitimate buyer in the USA.

---

### **III. Stage 1: NGJA Examination and Valuation Process**

This is the primary stage where the gemstones are physically inspected, identified, and valued by qualified gemologists of the NGJA.

1.  **Scheduling an Appointment:**
    *   The exporter must schedule an appointment with the NGJA Export Facilitation Unit well in advance. This ensures availability of expert gemologists and processing staff.
    *   Contact details: NGJA Head Office, No. 25, Galle Face Centre Road, Colombo 03, Sri Lanka.

2.  **Submission of Gemstones and Initial Documentation:**
    *   On the scheduled date, the exporter must physically bring the gemstones intended for export, securely packaged, to the NGJA Head Office.
    *   **MANDATORY DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED AT THIS STAGE:**
        *   **NGJA Export Application Form (Duly Filled):** Available at the NGJA office or website.
        *   **Commercial Invoice (Pro-forma or Final):** Detailing the buyer, seller, full description of goods (type, quantity, weight, individual price, total price), and Incoterms.
        *   **Packing List:** Itemizing each gemstone parcel with its specific weight, dimensions, and other relevant identifying marks.
        *   **Proof of Ownership/Sourcing:** Documents verifying the legal procurement of the gemstones (e.g., purchase receipts from miners/dealers, import documents if previously imported for cutting).
        *   **Company Registration Certificate:** Copy of business registration.
        *   **NGJA Exporter Registration Certificate:** Copy of valid NGJA registration.
        *   **Bank Account Details:** For foreign exchange repatriation purposes (linked to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka regulations).

3.  **NGJA Gemological Examination:**
    *   **Expert Gemologist Review:** The gemstones will be meticulously examined by NGJA's certified gemologists under controlled conditions.
    *   **Verification:** Cross-referencing the submitted documents (invoice, packing list) with the physical goods.
    *   **Identification:** Scientific identification of gemstone type, variety, and determination of natural vs. synthetic status.
    *   **Treatment Detection:** Identification of any treatments applied to the gemstones (e.g., heating, diffusion, fracture filling).
    *   **Quality Assessment:** Assessment of the "4 Cs" – Carat weight (actual weight verification), Color, Clarity, and Cut quality.
    *   **Origin (where possible/relevant):** While not always definitive for cut stones, any available information on origin will be noted.

4.  **Official Valuation:**
    *   Based on the gemological examination, NGJA experts will determine the Fair Market Value (FMV) of the gemstones.
    *   **Valuation Basis:** This valuation is based on current international market prices, quality, size, rarity, and prevailing industry standards. The NGJA valuation serves as the basis for calculating the export cess and for foreign exchange repatriation compliance.
    *   **Discrepancies:** In case of significant discrepancies between the exporter's declared value and the NGJA's valuation, discussions will be held with the exporter for clarification. The NGJA's valuation is final for regulatory purposes.

5.  **Sealing of Consignment:**
    *   Upon completion of examination and valuation, the gemstones will be securely packaged and sealed by NGJA officials. This seal must remain intact until cleared by Sri Lanka Customs.

---

### **IV. Stage 2: NGJA Export Permit Application and Issuance**

Once the examination and valuation are complete, the exporter proceeds to obtain the official Export Permit.

1.  **Payment of Export Cess/Levy:**
    *   An export cess (levy) is charged on the FOB (Free On Board) value of the exported gemstones, as determined by the NGJA. The applicable percentage will be informed by the NGJA finance division.
    *   Payment must be made to the NGJA cashier or through designated bank channels.

2.  **Application Review and Permit Generation:**
    *   The NGJA Export Facilitation Unit will review all documentation, the gemological report, valuation, and proof of cess payment.
    *   If all requirements are met, the official NGJA Export Permit will be generated.

3.  **Issuance of NGJA Export Permit:**
    *   The permit will contain critical information including:
        *   Exporter and Importer details
        *   Detailed description of the gemstones (type, weight, quantity, treatments, value)
        *   HS Code (7103.XX.XX)
        *   Destination Country (USA)
        *   NGJA Valuation and Cess paid
        *   Unique Permit Number
        *   Date of Issue and Validity Period (typically 30 days)
    *   **KEY DOCUMENT:** The original NGJA Export Permit is the primary regulatory document for export clearance.

---

### **V. Stage 3: Sri Lanka Customs Gem Unit Clearance**

With the NGJA Export Permit in hand, the exporter must now proceed to Sri Lanka Customs for final export clearance.

1.  **Engagement of a Licensed Customs House Agent (CHA):**
    *   It is highly recommended (and often mandatory for complex consignments) to engage a licensed Customs House Agent (CHA) to prepare and submit the necessary customs declarations.

2.  **Submission of Documents to Sri Lanka Customs Gem Unit:**
    *   The exporter or their appointed CHA must proceed to the dedicated Gem Unit at the Air Cargo Village, Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) or other designated Customs clearance points for sea cargo.
    *   **MANDATORY DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED TO CUSTOMS:**
        *   **Original NGJA Export Permit:** The most critical document.
        *   **Sri Lanka Customs Declaration (CUSDEC):** Electronically filed and printed, prepared by the CHA, detailing the export consignment.
        *   **Commercial Invoice:** Matching the details on the NGJA Permit.
        *   **Packing List:** Matching the details on the NGJA Permit.
        *   **Air Waybill (for Air Freight) / Bill of Lading (for Sea Freight):** Prepared by the freight forwarder.
        *   **Exporter's NGJA Registration & Business Registration:** Copies.
        *   **Any other supporting documents requested by Customs.**

3.  **Customs Verification and Inspection:**
    *   **Documentary Verification:** Customs officials will meticulously verify all submitted documents against the CUSDEC and especially against the NGJA Export Permit.
    *   **Physical Inspection (Discretionary):** The Customs Gem Unit reserves the right to physically inspect the consignment. This may involve:
        *   Verifying the integrity of the NGJA seal.
        *   Random or full re-examination of the gemstones to ensure they match the description on the NGJA Permit and CUSDEC.
        *   Confirmation of quantity, weight, and general conformity.
    *   **Valuation Review:** Customs will cross-reference the declared value with the NGJA valuation.

4.  **Payment of Other Applicable Customs Duties/Taxes (if any):**
    *   While export duties on cut and polished gems are typically minimal or zero, any other applicable charges (e.g., port charges, service fees) must be paid.

5.  **Customs Release and Endorsement:**
    *   Once Customs is satisfied with the documentation, inspection, and payment of any charges, they will endorse the NGJA Export Permit and other relevant documents, clearing the consignment for export.
    *   A Customs release stamp will be affixed, allowing the goods to proceed to the freight forwarder for onward shipment.

---

### **VI. Stage 4: Logistics and Shipment**

1.  **Handover to Freight Forwarder:**
    *   The cleared and sealed consignment, along with the endorsed Customs and NGJA documents, is handed over to the chosen reputable air cargo agent or courier service (e.g., FedEx, DHL, Brinks).
2.  **Insurance:**
    *   It is **highly recommended** to secure adequate insurance for the full value of the consignment against loss, theft, or damage during transit.
3.  **Secure Packaging:**
    *   Ensure the gemstones are packaged in tamper-proof, secure containers suitable for international air cargo.

---

### **VII. Stage 5: Post-Export Compliance**

1.  **Foreign Exchange Repatriation:**
    *   As per the regulations of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, the full export proceeds in foreign currency (USD in this case) must be repatriated to Sri Lanka through authorized banking channels within the stipulated timeframe (e.g., 90 or 180 days from the date of export, confirm with Central Bank guidelines).
    *   Proof of repatriation may be required by the NGJA and Central Bank.
2.  **Record Keeping:**
    *   Exporters must maintain complete and accurate records of all export transactions, including NGJA Permits, Customs documents, invoices, and bank statements, for a minimum period as required by law.

### **IX. Important Notes and Disclaimer**

*   **Changes in Regulations:** Exporters are advised to regularly check with the NGJA and Sri Lanka Customs for any updates or changes to the export procedures, cess rates, or required documentation.
*   **USA Import Regulations:** It is the sole responsibility of the **importer in the USA** to comply with all U.S. import regulations, duties, taxes, and any specific requirements set by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) or other relevant U.S. agencies.
*   **Ethical Sourcing:** The NGJA strongly encourages and expects all exporters to adhere to ethical sourcing practices.
*   **Contact Information:** For any clarifications or assistance, please contact the National Gem and Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka Export Facilitation Unit.
